Health advisory on social media use in adolescence

Author or publisher American Psychological Association Published 2023 Reviewed by Edustack editors Last checked 13 Nov 2026
Read the original · apa.org ↗

1What parents should know

Ten recommendations based on the research to date, including adult monitoring in early adolescence, protecting sleep and physical activity, and social media literacy training before use begins.

2What the source suggests you do

  • In early adolescence (about 10–14), adult monitoring is advised for most young people; allow more independence as they mature.
  • Keep social media from cutting into sleep (at least 8 hours) or physical activity. Use within an hour of bedtime is linked to poorer sleep.
  • Limit comparison-driven content, especially about appearance.
  • Teach social media literacy before your child starts using it.
