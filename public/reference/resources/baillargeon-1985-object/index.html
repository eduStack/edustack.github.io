Object permanence in five-month-old infants

Author or publisher Baillargeon, Spelke & Wasserman, Cognition Published 1985 Reviewed by Edustack editors Last checked 13 Nov 2026
Read the original · doi.org ↗

1What the study found

Five-month-old babies looked longer when a screen appeared to pass through a hidden box, suggesting they knew the box still existed - months earlier than Piaget's account predicted.
