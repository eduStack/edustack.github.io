The Scientist in the Crib: What Early Learning Tells Us About the Mind

Author or publisher Alison Gopnik, Andrew N. Meltzoff, Patricia K. Kuhl Published 1999 Reviewed by Edustack editors Last checked 13 Nov 2026
Read the original · openlibrary.org ↗Find in a library (WorldCat) ↗

Evidence: Three developmental scientists summarise infant research up to 1999 for parents. Newer studies have added detail since.

1What parents should know

Explains how babies and young children learn about people, objects and language by experimenting, much like small scientists.
