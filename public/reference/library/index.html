The international framework for early childhood. It names five components of nurturing care - good health, adequate nutrition, safety and security, responsive caregiving and opportunities for early learning.

Family Media Plan

An online tool from the American Academy of Pediatrics for agreeing screen and device rules as a family.

Guideline Evidence: Official guideline Ages 0–6
Media and Young Minds (AAP policy statement)

Under 18 months, avoid screen media other than video chatting. From 18 to 24 months, choose high-quality programmes and watch together. From 2 to 5 years, limit to 1 hour a day of high-quality programmes.

AAP Council on Communications and Media, Pediatrics·2016
Guideline Evidence: Official guideline 0–12 months
How to Keep Your Sleeping Baby Safe: AAP Policy Explained

Place babies on their back, on a firm flat surface of their own, with no soft objects, to lower the risk of sleep-related infant death.

American Academy of Pediatrics·2022
Guideline Evidence: Official guideline Ages 0–18
Effective Discipline to Raise Healthy Children (AAP clinical report)

The AAP advises against spanking, hitting, slapping, threatening, insulting or shaming children, and recommends teaching good behaviour through healthy forms of discipline instead.

American Academy of Pediatrics; Sege & Siegel, Pediatrics·2018

Explains how babies and young children learn about people, objects and language by experimenting, much like small scientists.

Guideline Evidence: Official guideline 2 months–6 years
CDC's Developmental Milestones (Learn the Signs. Act Early.)

Checklists of what most children can do at 12 ages from 2 months to 5 years, covering social, language, thinking and movement skills.

Guideline Evidence: Expert opinion 4 months–18 years
Recommended Amount of Sleep for Pediatric Populations: A Consensus Statement of the American Academy of Sleep Medicine

Expert panel recommendations for sleep per 24 hours: 12–16 hours at 4–12 months, 11–14 at 1–2 years, 10–13 at 3–5, 9–12 at 6–12 and 8–10 at 13–18.

Paruthi et al., Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine·2016
