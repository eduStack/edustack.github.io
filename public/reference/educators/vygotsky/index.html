Educators/Lev Vygotsky

Lev Vygotsky

1896–1934 · Russia (Soviet Union)

Russian psychologist whose 1934 book Thought and Language studied how thinking and speech develop together.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Thought and Language ↗

Core ideas

  • Children's thinking grows out of social interaction: they watch how others think and act, then make it their own.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Human intelligence: post-Piaget theories ↗
  • The "zone of proximal development": what a child can do with help today, they can do alone tomorrow.Vygotsky, Harvard University Press, 1978 · Mind in Society (library record) ↗
What it means for parents today: Help with just the part your child can't manage yet, then step back as they get it. Researchers call this "scaffolding".Wood, 1976

What modern research supports

  • Studies of adults tutoring young children described how well-judged help lets children finish tasks they couldn't do alone.Wood, 1976
  • Play with gentle adult guidance improved some early learning more than direct teaching.Skene et al., 2022
