Educators/Cai Yuanpei

Cai Yuanpei

1868–1940 · China

First minister of education of the Republic of China (1912) and head of Peking University from 1916 to 1926.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Cai Yuanpei ↗

Core ideas

  • Five kinds of education should all be developed together: physical and military, practical, civic morality, worldview, and aesthetic education.Wikisource · Cai Yuanpei, views on the new education (1912) ↗
  • Aesthetic education - art and beauty - can do for character what religion once did.Wikisource · Cai Yuanpei, replacing religion with aesthetic education (1917) ↗
What it means for parents today: Make room for movement, art and music alongside schoolwork.Wikisource · Cai Yuanpei, views on the new education (1912) ↗

What modern research supports

  • Today's guidelines also ask for daily physical activity: at least 60 minutes a day for ages 5–17.World Health Organization, 2020

What is outdated or needs correcting

  • He presented military-citizen education as a need of his time, given the threats China faced in 1912.Wikisource · Cai Yuanpei, views on the new education (1912) ↗
