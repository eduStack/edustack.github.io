Educators/John Dewey

John Dewey

1859–1952 · United States

American philosopher who founded the Laboratory School in Chicago to test ideas about education.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · John Dewey ↗

Core ideas

  • Education grows out of experience: children learn by doing and by working on real problems.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · John Dewey ↗
  • School should be connected to community and democratic life, not cut off from it.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · John Dewey ↗
What it means for parents today: Hands-on projects work well when an adult guides them: give feedback, show examples, and ask your child to explain their thinking.Alfieri et al., 2011

What modern research supports

  • Discovery and project learning with support beats both lectures and unguided discovery.Alfieri et al., 2011

What is outdated or needs correcting

  • Versions of "learning by doing" that give beginners little guidance do not work well.Kirschner, 2006
