Educators/Maria Montessori

Maria Montessori

1870–1952 · Italy

Italian physician and educator who developed the Montessori method in the early 1900s.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Montessori schools ↗

Core ideas

  • Children choose their own activities and move freely, rather than sitting at desks.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Montessori schools ↗
  • Specially designed hands-on materials that appeal to the senses.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Montessori schools ↗
  • Children are challenged according to their ability, and help each other learn.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Montessori schools ↗
What it means for parents today: At home this can mean letting children do real tasks for themselves and choose between a few well-chosen activities.Encyclopaedia Britannica · Montessori schools ↗

What modern research supports

  • A systematic review of 32 studies found Montessori pupils did somewhat better, academically and in executive function.Randolph et al., 2023
  • In a lottery-based study, Montessori preschool narrowed the gap between lower- and higher-income children.Lillard et al., 2017

What is outdated or needs correcting

  • Benefits were larger in preschool and primary than in secondary school, and some outcomes rest on low-quality evidence.Randolph et al., 2023
