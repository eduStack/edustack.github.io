Educators/Jean-Jacques Rousseau

Jean-Jacques Rousseau

1712–1778 · Geneva and France

Philosopher whose novel-treatise Emile set out an education that follows the child's natural development.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · Jean Jacques Rousseau ↗

Core ideas

  • Child-centred "negative education": education should work in harmony with the development of the child's natural capacities.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · Jean Jacques Rousseau ↗
  • Children should learn from experience and discovery more than from books and lectures.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · Jean Jacques Rousseau ↗
What it means for parents today: Let children try things for themselves at a level that fits their age, while staying close enough to help.Alfieri et al., 2011

What modern research supports

  • Discovery learning works when it comes with support such as feedback and examples.Alfieri et al., 2011

What is outdated or needs correcting

  • Leaving children to discover things entirely alone teaches less than explicit teaching.Alfieri et al., 2011
  • Book V of Emile says a woman "is made to please and to be in subjection to man" - a view of girls' education modern readers reject.Project Gutenberg · Emile (Foxley translation), Book V ↗
