Confucius

551–479 BCE (traditional dates) · China

Teacher and thinker of ancient China whose sayings were collected by his students in the Analects.Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · Confucius ↗

Core ideas

  • Learning and thinking go together: "Learning without thinking is useless; thinking without learning is dangerous."Wikisource · Analects, Book 2 (Chinese text) ↗
  • Teach each student according to who they are: asked the same question by two students, he gave them opposite advice. Later scholars summed this up as "teaching according to aptitude" (因材施教).Wikisource · Analects, Book 11 (Chinese text) ↗
  • Education is for everyone: "In teaching there should be no distinction of classes."Wikisource · Analects, Book 15 (Chinese text) ↗
What it means for parents today: Notice what your child already knows and how they think, and adjust how you help, rather than using one approach for every child.Wikisource · Analects, Book 11 (Chinese text) ↗

What modern research supports

  • Teaching children at their actual level, rather than strictly by grade, raised learning in large randomised trials.Banerjee et al., 2017
