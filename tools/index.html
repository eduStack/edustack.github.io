Free learning tools

Free learning tools to use with your child. Age ranges come from each tool's own guidance. Every tool has to meet the criteria below.

How we choose tools

  1. Free: Core content is free to use, with no subscription.
  2. No ads: No advertising found on the site or app when we last checked.
  3. Children's data: A public privacy policy or parents' page. We link it so you can judge for yourself; we do not vouch for it.
  4. Research or institutional backing: Built by a university or non-profit, or publishes research and evaluations.
  5. Checked monthly: Every link is checked automatically each month. Tools that break or stop meeting these criteria are removed.
