The association between adolescent well-being and digital technology use

Author or publisher Orben & Przybylski, Nature Human Behaviour Published 2019 Reviewed by Edustack editors Last checked 13 Nov 2026
Read the original · doi.org ↗

1What the study found

Using data on more than 350,000 adolescents, the study found that digital technology use explained at most 0.4% of the variation in well-being - a negative but very small association on average.
