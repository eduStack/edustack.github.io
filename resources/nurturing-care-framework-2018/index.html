Nurturing Care Framework for Early Childhood Development

Author or publisher WHO, UNICEF, World Bank Group Published 2018 Reviewed by Edustack editors Last checked 13 Nov 2026
Read the original · nurturing-care.org ↗

1What parents should know

The international framework for early childhood. It names five components of nurturing care - good health, adequate nutrition, safety and security, responsive caregiving and opportunities for early learning.

2Source and checks

The research behind it

What each study found comes first, then the kind of study and where it was published. We prefer systematic reviews and meta-analyses, and check for new research every month.

  • In a study of 4- to 6-year-olds, the number of back-and-forth conversational turns with adults predicted activity in language areas of the brain better than the number of words children heard.

    Single-time studyRomeo et al., Psychological Science, 2018Study details

  • 9-month-old infants learned Mandarin speech sounds from live sessions with a person, but not from the same material shown on video or audio.

    ExperimentKuhl, Tsao & Liu, PNAS, 2003Study details
