Effective Discipline to Raise Healthy Children (AAP clinical report)

Author or publisher American Academy of Pediatrics; Sege & Siegel, Pediatrics Published 2018 Reviewed by Edustack editors Last checked 13 Nov 2026
Read the original · doi.org ↗

1What parents should know

The AAP advises against spanking, hitting, slapping, threatening, insulting or shaming children, and recommends teaching good behaviour through healthy forms of discipline instead.

The research behind it

What each study found comes first, then the kind of study and where it was published. We prefer systematic reviews and meta-analyses, and check for new research every month.

  • Meta-analyses of 75 studies found spanking was linked to a range of poor outcomes for children and was not linked to better behaviour.
